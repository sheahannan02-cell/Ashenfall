How this version works

Ashenfall v0.2

Move: left joystick. Basic attack: sword button. Skills: the three small ability buttons. Dash and Ultimate sit around them.

Enemies continuously scale in health, damage and density. Completing each 20-kill Hunt increases World Tier and improves loot quality.

Weapons are locked to your class. Legendary and Unique items can massively alter a specific ability. Sets are deliberately short, usually 2-3 pieces, so you can mix them with ability-defining gear.

The Ability Evolution screen lets you spend points on the selected skill. The Great Web is shared across all classes.
