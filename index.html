How this version works

Ashenfall v0.4.5

Sanctuary: the green central circle is safe and rapidly heals you. Leave it when you are ready to enter the endless hunt.

Move: left joystick. Basic attack: sword button. Skills: tap the three ability buttons, Dash or Ultimate. A dark radial gauge and number show the remaining cooldown; a green rim means the ability is ready.

Combat happens outside the Sanctuary as an endless hunt. Enemies spawn continuously and randomly until the screen becomes packed, while bosses periodically enter the fight without replacing the horde. Heavy enemies move slowly and telegraph dangerous attacks.

Weapons are locked to your class. Legendary and Unique items can massively alter a specific ability. Sets are deliberately short, usually 2-3 pieces, so you can mix them with ability-defining gear.

The Ability Evolution screen develops individual skills. The Great Web is now a long-term progression system with branches, multi-point nodes, capstones and a 100-point Apex.
